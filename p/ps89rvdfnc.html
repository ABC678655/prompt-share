📝 提示词

CHARACTER INFORMATION — EDIT ONLY THIS SECTION



CHINESE NAME: [xx]



ENGLISH NAME: [xx]



FACTION: [xx]



Use the provided character reference as the primary and authoritative visual identity reference.



The Chinese name, English name, and faction defined at the beginning of this prompt are the ONLY canonical identity information for the artwork.



Whenever the character's name appears visually in the final artwork, use exactly the Chinese and English names defined above.



Whenever a faction emblem or faction-related visual identity appears, use the faction defined above.



Do not invent alternate names.

Do not invent alternate spellings.

Do not introduce unrelated character names.

Do not introduce an unrelated faction.

Do not replace the defined names with placeholder text.



Create a premium vertical Zenless Zone Zero character editorial poster inspired by elegant torn-paper collage art, luxury anime key visuals, collectible game merchandise, sophisticated art-book covers, premium stationery design, celestial graphic motifs, and restrained fashion-editorial composition.



The final result should feel like an official limited-edition Zenless Zone Zero character art card rather than an ordinary anime illustration.



ASPECT RATIO:

9:16 vertical poster.



\==================================================



1. CHARACTER IDENTITY — HIGHEST PRIORITY

   \==================================================



Faithfully preserve the provided reference character.



The reference image is the highest-priority and authoritative source for the character's visual identity.



Preserve accurately:



- facial identity,

- face shape,

- eye shape,

- exact eye color,

- eyebrow shape,

- nose and mouth proportions,

- hairstyle,

- hair color,

- bangs,

- side locks,

- rear hair structure,

- signature hair silhouette,

- characteristic hair accessories,

- headwear if present,

- earrings or jewelry if present,

- neck accessories,

- recognizable costume elements,

- characteristic costume motifs,

- signature decorations,

- characteristic color palette,

- body proportions,

- overall recognizable identity.



Do not redesign the character into a generic anime girl.



Do not simplify away identity-defining features.



Do not replace distinctive accessories with generic decorations.



The character must remain immediately recognizable as the reference character even though only part of the face and upper head may be visible through the torn-paper opening.



\==================================================

2\. CORE ART DIRECTION

======================



Create a sophisticated layered-paper editorial composition.



The artwork consists primarily of two large sheets of premium handmade art paper:



- a pale upper paper layer,

- a darker lower paper layer.



Between these two sheets is an irregular horizontal torn opening.



Through this opening, reveal a close-up portrait of the character.



The character should appear physically behind the paper layers, as though the paper has been torn open to reveal another world or portrait underneath.



The composition should feel elegant, quiet, mysterious, poetic, premium, collectible, and carefully art-directed.



Avoid a scrapbook or casual collage appearance.



This is luxury editorial graphic design combined with premium anime/game illustration.



\==================================================

3\. TORN-PAPER WINDOW

=====================



Create one major irregular horizontal tear running across most of the width of the poster.



The torn opening should occupy approximately 20–30% of the total vertical canvas.



Its shape should be organic and asymmetric.



The upper and lower edges should have different contours.



Use:



- irregular paper fibers,

- natural torn edges,

- subtle paper thickness,

- slight curling,

- small variations in depth,

- occasional tiny protruding fibers,

- believable physical separation between paper layers.



The opening should NOT look like a straight rectangular crop.



Avoid a perfectly horizontal slit.



Avoid smooth vector curves.



Avoid symmetrical tearing.



The character should feel hidden behind the paper rather than pasted onto the surface.



\==================================================

4\. CHARACTER FRAMING

=====================



Use a close-up anime/game portrait.



The torn opening should primarily reveal:



- the eyes,

- the upper face,

- bangs,

- characteristic side hair,

- signature head accessories,

- and optionally the nose, cheeks, and mouth depending on what best preserves the character's identity.



The eyes should be the strongest visual focal point in the entire character area.



Use either:



- near-frontal view,

- or a subtle three-quarter angle.



The pose should remain calm and restrained.



The character may look:



- directly toward the viewer,

- slightly past the viewer,

- or slightly to one side.



Expression:



quiet,

gentle,

slightly distant,

poised,

introspective,

subtly mysterious,

emotionally restrained,

softly confident.



A very subtle smile is acceptable when appropriate to the reference character.



Avoid:



- exaggerated laughter,

- wide-open mouth,

- energetic idol expressions,

- exaggerated surprise,

- extreme sadness,

- exaggerated seductive expression,

- exaggerated action poses.



The visual language should resemble a poetic character portrait captured for a premium art book.



\==================================================

5\. CHARACTER-ADAPTIVE COLOR SYSTEM

===================================



IMPORTANT:



Do NOT automatically use the blue-and-white palette from the example artwork.



Analyze the provided character reference first.



Identify three major color roles:



PRIMARY COLOR:

the strongest dominant identity color of the character.



SECONDARY COLOR:

the main supporting color found in the hair, clothing, accessories, eyes, or costume.



ACCENT COLOR:

a smaller distinctive highlight color associated with the character.



Build the entire poster palette around these colors.



The final palette must look intentionally designed specifically for this character.



Do not force unrelated blue tones onto non-blue characters.



Do not force gold onto every character unless it visually harmonizes with the character's design.



\==================================================

6\. UPPER PAPER COLOR

=====================



The upper paper should use a very pale, elegant and desaturated interpretation of the character's palette.



Possible treatments include:



- soft ivory,

- warm pearl white,

- cool pearl white,

- pale cream,

- misty gray,

- desaturated pastel derived from the PRIMARY color,

- very pale version of the SECONDARY color.



Keep this region light and spacious.



Use very gentle tonal variation rather than a perfectly flat digital fill.



The upper region should create large areas of calm negative space.



\==================================================

7\. LOWER PAPER COLOR

=====================



The lower paper should use a deeper, muted and elegant version of the character's dominant palette.



Depending on the character, suitable tones may resemble:



- midnight navy,

- dark indigo,

- smoky violet,

- muted burgundy,

- wine red,

- deep rose,

- dark teal,

- forest green,

- muted emerald,

- charcoal,

- deep warm brown,

- blue-black,

- violet-black,

- character-specific deep neutral tones.



The lower paper should visually anchor the composition.



It should provide sufficient contrast for character-name typography and faction branding.



\==================================================

8\. METALLIC TORN EDGE

======================



Add an extremely thin metallic treatment along selected portions of the torn-paper edges.



Choose the metallic color according to the character palette.



Possible choices:



- champagne gold,

- antique pale gold,

- muted rose gold,

- pale silver,

- platinum,

- cool metallic white.



The foil should look physically applied by hand.



Use:



- irregular thickness,

- broken sections,

- tiny gaps,

- subtle reflective highlights,

- uneven metallic accumulation,

- delicate handmade imperfections.



The foil must NOT appear as a clean vector outline.



It should not form a continuous glowing border.



Keep it restrained, refined and premium.



Avoid bright yellow-gold cartoon outlines.



\==================================================

9\. LIGHTING

============



Use soft cinematic anime portrait lighting.



The main light may appear to enter subtly through the torn opening.



Use:



- soft directional illumination,

- delicate highlights on the cheeks,

- subtle nose bridge highlight,

- controlled highlights on important hair locks,

- clear luminous reflections inside the eyes,

- extremely subtle rim lighting where appropriate,

- soft facial shadow transitions.



The face should be slightly brighter than the surrounding portrait environment.



The lighting should emphasize the eyes without creating harsh contrast.



Maintain soft atmospheric depth behind the character.



Avoid:



- neon lighting,

- cyberpunk lighting,

- rainbow lighting,

- extreme bloom,

- aggressive lens flare,

- harsh studio flash,

- hard spotlight,

- excessive rim lighting,

- blown highlights.



\==================================================

10\. BACKGROUND GRAPHIC LANGUAGE

================================



Decorate the paper surfaces with extremely subtle line-art ornaments.



These elements should feel printed, embossed, engraved, or faintly stamped into the paper.



Possible motifs:



- tiny four-point stars,

- small celestial sparkles,

- constellations,

- orbital circles,

- crescent forms,

- astronomical diagrams,

- star charts,

- subtle geometric sigils,

- delicate floral line art,

- very thin ornamental curves,

- fine decorative vertical lines,

- restrained game-interface-inspired geometry.



Keep these motifs extremely faint.



They should normally use only a slightly darker or lighter variation of the surrounding paper color.



They must remain subordinate to:



1. the character's eyes,

2. the torn-paper opening,

3. the character-name typography.



Do not create a busy magical background.



Do not fill every empty area.



Negative space is essential.



\==================================================

11\. ZENLESS ZONE ZERO BRANDING

===============================



Include one tasteful Zenless Zone Zero logo.



Place it within an appropriate negative-space area.



Preferred locations include:



- upper-right,

- upper-left,

- near one upper edge,

- or another quiet editorial position.



The logo should remain clearly readable but relatively small.



Adapt its monochrome treatment to the overall character palette.



Possible treatments:



- dark ink on pale paper,

- pale ink on dark paper,

- subtle metallic printing,

- soft character-accent color.



The Zenless Zone Zero logo must remain secondary to the character portrait.



Do not:



- enlarge it excessively,

- repeat it multiple times,

- place it over the eyes,

- place it over the face,

- use a giant advertising-style logo.



\==================================================

12\. CHARACTER NAME TYPOGRAPHY

==============================



Display both canonical names defined at the beginning of the prompt.



The English name should normally function as the main typography element.



The Chinese name should appear as a smaller supporting element.



Use the names exactly as provided.



Do not translate them.

Do not abbreviate them.

Do not invent additional spellings.



Typography style:



premium anime key visual,

luxury editorial design,

fashion magazine typography,

art-book cover typography,

high-end game merchandise branding.



The English name may use:



- elegant high-contrast serif,

- refined modern serif,

- sophisticated contemporary display type,

- restrained uppercase lettering,

- generous tracking,

- widely spaced letters.



The Chinese name should use:



- clean elegant Chinese typography,

- refined Song-style visual language,

- restrained modern Chinese display typography,

- or another style harmonizing with the character.



Maintain strong typographic hierarchy.



Recommended hierarchy:



large English character name,

smaller Chinese character name,

faction emblem beneath.



Preferred positioning:



- lower-center,

- lower-left,

- lower-right,

- or another spacious area in the lower third.



Do not cover the character's eyes.



Do not place major typography directly over the face.



Use text colors derived from the adaptive palette.



On dark backgrounds:



use pale ivory,

soft pearl,

light PRIMARY variation,

light SECONDARY variation,

or restrained metallic ACCENT.



On pale backgrounds:



use deep PRIMARY,

deep SECONDARY,

muted charcoal,

or another elegant dark character-derived tone.



The typography should look professionally typeset rather than randomly generated.



\==================================================

13\. FACTION EMBLEM

===================



Replace the small generic decorative symbol normally found near the bottom of this style of poster with the actual emblem associated with the faction defined at the beginning of the prompt.



The emblem should function as a refined official branding element.



Place it in the lower portion of the composition, preferably around the lower-center region beneath the character names.



Recommended arrangement:



thin horizontal decorative line

— faction emblem —

thin horizontal decorative line



Keep the emblem recognizable but restrained.



Adapt its treatment to the overall palette.



Possible visual treatments:



- fine metallic ink,

- champagne-gold foil,

- pale silver foil,

- subtle embossed ink,

- pale character-accent printing,

- elegant monochrome printing.



The emblem must belong to the defined faction.



Do not invent an unrelated random crest.



Do not substitute a generic star or flower when an identifiable faction emblem is available from the character/reference information.



The faction emblem should replace the small ornamental logo found in the example poster compositions.



\==================================================

14\. EDITORIAL LAYOUT

=====================



Use a strong vertical hierarchy.



Approximate composition:



TOP 30–40%:



large pale negative-space region,

subtle celestial or ornamental graphics,

small Zenless Zone Zero logo,

quiet premium-paper atmosphere.



MIDDLE 20–30%:



irregular torn-paper opening,

character close-up,

eyes as the dominant focal point,

signature hair and accessories visible.



LOWER 35–45%:



deep character-adaptive paper region,

large elegant negative space,

English character name,

smaller Chinese character name,

faction emblem,

minimal decorative lines and celestial details.



Keep the overall composition asymmetrical enough to feel designed rather than mechanically centered.



The character opening may be slightly higher or lower depending on the character's hairstyle and visual identity.



Do not overcrowd the lower typography area.



Do not attempt to fill all empty space.



Large controlled negative space is an intentional design feature.



\==================================================

15\. PAPER MATERIAL

===================



Use premium handmade fine-art paper.



The paper should have:



- subtle physical fibers,

- extremely gentle tactile texture,

- soft natural tonal variation,

- light watercolor-like pigment transitions,

- slight surface depth,

- delicate handmade imperfections.



The texture must remain subtle.



The paper should feel expensive and refined rather than old or damaged.



Think:



premium stationery,

luxury art print,

museum-quality character card,

high-end book-cover stock.



Do NOT use:



heavy grain,

dirty fibers,

grunge texture,

aged parchment stains,

scratches,

dust,

rough canvas,

sand-like texture,

extreme watercolor granulation.



\==================================================

16\. DEPTH AND LAYERING

=======================



Create believable physical separation between:



foreground upper paper,

character portrait layer,

foreground lower paper.



Use extremely subtle shadows around the torn edges.



The paper should cast a faint natural shadow onto the portrait underneath.



Do not create heavy drop shadows.



Use shallow physical depth similar to real layered paper photographed under soft studio lighting.



Metallic foil should catch small controlled highlights.



The character remains visually behind the torn-paper layers.



\==================================================

17\. ATMOSPHERE

===============



Overall emotional direction:



quiet,

poetic,

dreamlike,

mysterious,

elegant,

restrained,

introspective,

ethereal,

slightly distant,

softly melancholic when appropriate,

luxurious,

timeless,

collectible.



The artwork should communicate character personality through subtle expression and color rather than action.



It should feel more like:



a luxury character portrait,

a limited-edition art card,

a premium art-book cover,

a sophisticated game key visual,

a collectible character poster,



rather than a standard promotional splash illustration.



\==================================================

18\. ANIME / GAME RENDERING

===========================



Render the character as a premium anime/game illustration.



Use:



refined anime facial construction,

accurate symmetrical eye placement,

beautiful expressive eyes,

precise eyelashes,

smooth skin,

controlled blush,

soft but defined facial shadows,

clean hair masses,

large coherent hair locks,

carefully selected individual strands,

controlled highlights,

precise accessories,

refined costume details.



Use a slight painterly influence only in secondary transitions.



The face and eyes must remain exceptionally clean and precise.



Do not make the character photorealistic.



Do not create realistic skin pores.



Do not turn the character into a realistic human cosplayer.



Maintain polished anime/game character aesthetics.



\==================================================

19\. VISUAL FOCUS

=================



Focal priority should be:



1. character eyes and facial identity,

2. irregular torn-paper opening,

3. character names,

4. faction emblem,

5. Zenless Zone Zero logo,

6. subtle celestial decoration.



Maintain this hierarchy strictly.



Decorative elements must never overpower the character.



\==================================================

20\. CLEAN RENDERING / IMAGE QUALITY

====================================



Extremely clean, polished anime/game illustration finish.



Use:



smooth controlled color transitions,

clean cel-influenced shading,

precise edges,

clear facial features,

large coherent color areas,

clean controlled gradients,

smooth anime-style skin,

well-defined hair masses,

clean coherent hair locks,

precise eyelashes,

controlled shadow shapes,

high visual clarity.



The character portrait should be cleaner than the surrounding paper texture.



Keep the eyes crystal-clear and visually focused.



Hair should be constructed from coherent locks rather than thousands of random noisy strands.



Skin should use smooth anime-style shading without pores or granular microtexture.



Shadows should be clean, smooth, deliberate and clearly shaped.



The paper may have subtle tactile texture, but this texture must remain fine, restrained and visually clean.



Avoid:



digital noise,

film grain,

chromatic grain,

luminance grain,

random colored pixels,

dirty speckles,

dust,

sand-like texture,

rough canvas texture,

heavy paper grain,

random microtexture,

excessive skin texture,

skin pores,

noisy hair strands,

muddy shadows,

muddy gradients,

dirty color transitions,

blotchy shading,

chromatic noise,

JPEG artifacts,

compression artifacts,

oversharpening halos,

excessive micro-contrast,

edge ringing,

artificial HDR,

plastic skin.



\==================================================

21\. TYPOGRAPHY ACCURACY

========================



Typography accuracy is important.



Render only the necessary character and branding text.



Prioritize correct spelling and clean readability.



Do not create random paragraphs.



Do not create pseudo-English filler.



Do not generate Lorem Ipsum.



Do not add unnecessary quotes, slogans, artist credits, prompt credits or fake production notes.



Do not include:



“PROMPT BY”

“REFERENCE-INSPIRED EDITORIAL”



or similar creator-credit text from the stylistic examples.



The lower information area should remain clean and character-focused.



\==================================================

22\. FINAL IMAGE TARGET

=======================



9:16 vertical composition.



Ultra-high-resolution premium anime editorial poster.



Professional print-quality finish.



Highly accurate character identity.



Elegant character-adaptive color system.



Sophisticated upper-light / lower-dark paper hierarchy.



Organic torn-paper window.



Subtle metallic foil torn edges.



Beautiful expressive eyes.



Tasteful Zenless Zone Zero logo integration.



Accurate English and Chinese character names.



Correct faction emblem replacing the generic lower decorative symbol.



Large intentional negative space.



Subtle celestial graphic details.



Premium handmade-paper material.



Clean, refined, collectible official-game-merchandise feeling.



The final artwork should feel as though it belongs to a coherent official Zenless Zone Zero limited-edition character poster collection, while allowing each individual character's original color palette, faction identity, hairstyle, personality and visual design to determine the final appearance.
