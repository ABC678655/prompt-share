Create a vertical minimalist Zenless Zone Zero character fashion portrait.



The target character is presented in a relaxed casual / lounge-fashion alternate outfit.



The design concept should preserve the strongest visual principle of the style reference:



A CLEAN FITTED UPPER SILHOUETTE

contrasted with

A SOFT LOOSE LOWER SILHOUETTE.



However:



DO NOT copy the exact clothing from the style reference.



The new outfit must be designed specifically for the TARGET CHARACTER.



The clothing colors,

small design motifs,

personality,

The overall image should feel:



minimal,

cool,

casual,

fashionable,

character-focused,

slightly aloof,

clean,

and understated.



ASPECT RATIO:

9:16 vertical.





==================================================

1. REFERENCE ROLE SEPARATION — CRITICAL

==================================================



TARGET CHARACTER REFERENCE controls:



facial identity,

face shape,

canonical eye shape,

canonical eye color,

hairstyle,

hair color,

bangs,

side locks,

rear hair structure,

ahoge if present,

signature hair silhouette,

recognizable accessories,

character personality,

body proportions,

canonical color palette,

signature motifs,

faction-inspired design language,

and overall recognizable identity.



STYLE REFERENCE controls ONLY:



minimalist portrait composition,

fitted-upper / loose-lower outfit philosophy,

simple background,

subtle low-angle camera feeling,

clean fashion-poster presentation,

restrained body language,

minimal graphic lighting.



Do NOT copy the style-reference character's:



face,

black-and-red hairstyle,

red eyes,

hairpins,

exact pose,

exact upper garment,

exact pants,

exact colors.



The target character must remain immediately recognizable.





==================================================

2. CHARACTER IDENTITY — HIGHEST PRIORITY

==================================================



Faithfully preserve the TARGET CHARACTER.



Preserve:



face identity,

face proportions,

canonical eye color,

eye shape,

hairstyle topology,

hair color,

signature bangs,

side locks,

important hair accessories,

head accessories,

recognizable jewelry where appropriate,

body proportions,

visual personality,

and characteristic color identity.



Do not redesign the character into a generic fashion-model anime girl.



The fashion redesign must clearly still belong to this specific character.





==================================================

3. REMOVE ORIGINAL OFFICIAL CLOTHING

==================================================



Do NOT preserve the target character's original official clothing literally.



Do NOT use:



original combat outfit,

canonical uniform,

battle gear,

armor,

official coat,

original dress,

original tactical clothing,

original costume silhouette.



The target character reference supplies DESIGN LANGUAGE,

not the final clothing.



Create NEW casual clothing.





==================================================

4. OUTFIT CONCEPT — CRITICAL

==================================================



Design a completely NEW character-specific casual / lounge outfit.



The main silhouette principle is:



UPPER BODY:

clean,

fitted,

minimal,

close to the body.



LOWER BODY:

loose,

soft,

relaxed,

casual.



This contrast must remain visually clear.



The result should look like contemporary character-specific casual fashion,

not battle clothing,

not formal clothing,

and not sleepwear.





==================================================

5. UPPER GARMENT — NEW DESIGN

==================================================



Create a NEW fitted sleeveless or short-sleeve upper garment.



Do NOT reproduce the exact high-neck halter bodysuit from the style reference.



Choose ONE character-appropriate variation such as:



asymmetrical mock-neck fitted top,

minimal sleeveless technical top,

soft high-neck fitted lounge top,

one-shoulder structured casual top,

minimal fitted tank with geometric neckline,

clean sleeveless fitted shirt,

character-inspired athletic fashion top.



The garment should remain:



simple,

close-fitting,

clean,

modern,

and visually uninterrupted.



Avoid excessive layers.





==================================================

6. UPPER GARMENT DIFFERENCE CHECK

==================================================



The final upper garment must differ visibly from the style-reference clothing.



Change at least several of:



neckline,

shoulder construction,

side paneling,

hem shape,

seam placement,

material blocking,

small character-specific detailing.



Do NOT reproduce:



the exact black high-neck halter shape,

the exact arm strap,

the exact side opening,

the exact torso construction.



The style reference provides only the GENERAL FASHION CATEGORY.





==================================================

7. LOWER GARMENT

==================================================



Use relaxed loose-fitting casual pants.



Possible character-adaptive types:



wide-leg drawstring lounge pants,

soft straight-leg sweatpants,

relaxed tapered pants,

minimal technical lounge trousers,

wide casual training pants.



The lower garment should visibly contrast with the fitted upper garment.



Use:



soft volume,

broad folds,

relaxed waist,

comfortable silhouette.



Do not make the pants skin-tight.





==================================================

8. PANTS DESIGN DIFFERENCE

==================================================



Do NOT reproduce the exact white sweatpants from the style reference.



Adapt:



waist construction,

leg width,

cuff style,

pocket design,

seam placement,

drawstring form,

and color.



Possible alternatives:



flat elastic waistband,

small drawcord,

side seam pocket,

minimal piping,

subtle asymmetric panel,

character-specific accent stitching.



Keep the design restrained.





==================================================

9. CHARACTER-ADAPTIVE COLOR EXTRACTION

==================================================



Analyze the TARGET CHARACTER and identify:



PRIMARY COLOR:

the strongest visual identity color.



SECONDARY COLOR:

the major supporting character color.



ACCENT COLOR:

a smaller signature color from eyes,

accessories,

faction motifs,

or costume details.



Build the casual outfit around these colors.



Do NOT automatically use:



black upper garment,

white pants,

pink outline.



Those colors belong only to the style reference.





==================================================

10. OUTFIT COLOR STRUCTURE

==================================================



Use approximately:



50–65%:

PRIMARY or dark-neutral character-adaptive color.



25–40%:

SECONDARY or light-neutral character-adaptive color.



5–10%:

ACCENT COLOR.



A good strategy:



UPPER GARMENT:

deeper / more concentrated version of PRIMARY.



LOWER GARMENT:

lighter,

softer,

or more neutral interpretation of SECONDARY.



ACCENT:

small seams,

drawstring,

trim,

tiny fastener,

or outline detail.



Maintain strong upper/lower tonal contrast.





==================================================

11. CHARACTER-SPECIFIC FASHION LANGUAGE

==================================================



Adapt the clothing design according to personality.



For a calm / introverted character:



use softer shapes,

subtle colors,

clean neckline,

minimal decoration.



For a cool / aloof character:



use sharper geometry,

darker tones,

asymmetric seam structure,

restrained technical details.



For an energetic character:



use brighter accent,

sportier cut,

lighter fabric,

slightly more relaxed silhouette.



For an elegant character:



use refined neckline,

smooth drape,

subtle asymmetric shaping,

soft premium-looking fabric.



For a playful character:



use one or two small character-specific graphic details,

contrast drawstring,

small color-blocking accent.



Do NOT treat all characters identically.





==================================================

12. OUTFIT DESIGN RESTRAINT

==================================================



Keep clothing detail limited.



Use:



large clean fabric regions,

few seams,

few decorative elements,

one or two signature character details.



Do NOT add:



many belts,

armor pieces,

complex harnesses,

multiple buckles,

chains everywhere,

excessive metallic hardware,

combat pouches,

large faction graphics.



This is casual fashion.





==================================================

13. POSE SELECTION BASED ON PERSONALITY

==================================================



Choose the pose according to the personality visually communicated by the TARGET CHARACTER reference.



The pose must remain simple and fashion-oriented.



Select ONE natural pose family.



CALM / RESERVED:

both hands loosely behind the back,

shoulders relaxed,

slight head tilt,

weight subtly shifted.



COOL / ALOOF:

one hand resting casually in a pants pocket,

other arm relaxed,

chin slightly raised,

subtle sideward gaze.



ENERGETIC / PLAYFUL:

one hand lightly touching the waistband or drawstring,

body leaning forward only slightly,

lively but controlled expression.



ELEGANT:

hands loosely joined behind the back,

gentle body curve,

slight three-quarter torso angle,

composed expression.



LAID-BACK:

one hand in a pocket,

one shoulder slightly lowered,

relaxed asymmetrical posture.



Do not use an action pose.





==================================================

14. POSE MUST MATCH CHARACTER

==================================================



Do NOT mechanically reproduce the exact arms-behind-back pose from the style reference.



The final pose should feel psychologically consistent with the target character.



The personality should be readable through:



shoulder position,

head tilt,

hand placement,

weight distribution,

gaze direction.



Keep all movements restrained.





==================================================

15. BODY ORIENTATION

==================================================



Use a mostly frontal or subtle three-quarter body orientation.



Recommended torso rotation:



0–20 degrees.



Do not use a dramatic profile.



Do not show the character from behind.



Keep both sides of the torso largely readable.



The outfit silhouette should remain clear.





==================================================

16. CAMERA ANGLE

==================================================



Use a subtle LOW-ANGLE portrait.



The camera sits slightly below the character's natural eye line.



Approximately:



5–10 degrees upward.



This should create only a mild sense of presence and confidence.



Do NOT create:



extreme worm's-eye perspective,

heroic low-angle distortion,

exaggerated chest / hip perspective.



Keep anatomy natural.





==================================================

17. CAMERA DISTANCE

==================================================



Use a medium-long portrait crop.



Show approximately:



head

to

upper thighs.



Do not show the entire body.



Do not show feet.



This framing preserves the fashion silhouette while keeping the face important.





==================================================

18. CHARACTER SCALE

==================================================



The character should occupy approximately:



80–88% of canvas height.



Keep the figure large and dominant.



Use only minimal empty space around the silhouette.





==================================================

19. FACIAL EXPRESSION

==================================================



Adapt expression to the target character.



Keep it restrained.



Suitable expression range:



neutral,

slightly aloof,

quiet,

self-assured,

relaxed,

mildly bored,

gentle,

subtly amused,

softly confident.



Avoid:



huge smile,

open-mouth grin,

exaggerated blush,

crying,

surprise,

action-scene intensity.



The fashion portrait should feel composed.





==================================================

20. GAZE

==================================================



Choose gaze based on personality.



Possible:



direct gaze for confident characters,



slightly downward gaze for aloof characters,



slightly off-camera gaze for reserved characters,



subtle side gaze for playful characters.



Do not use exaggerated eye direction.



The eyes should remain clearly readable.





==================================================

21. BACKGROUND — MINIMAL

==================================================



Use an extremely simple background.



Preferred:



clean pale neutral background,

soft off-white,

very pale warm gray,

very pale cool gray,

or a barely tinted character-adaptive neutral.



No environment.



No room.



No furniture.



No scenery.



No landscape.



No city.



The background should function only as a clean character-display field.





==================================================

22. BACKGROUND COLOR ADAPTATION

==================================================



Adapt the background subtly to the character.



If the character palette is cool:



use pale cool gray,

soft blue-white,

pale lavender-gray.



If the palette is warm:



use warm ivory,

pale beige-gray,

soft peach-white.



If the character is very dark:



use a moderately light neutral background for silhouette separation.



Keep saturation extremely low.





==================================================

23. BACKGROUND CLEANLINESS

==================================================



The background must remain perfectly clean.



No:



grain,

paper texture,

canvas,

wall texture,

brush texture,

random gradient artifacts,

dust,

noise,

decorative patterns.



Use a smooth flat or extremely gentle gradient.





==================================================

24. LIGHTING

==================================================



Use simple soft character-portrait lighting.



Primary:



large soft frontal / slightly upper-front light.



Secondary:



very subtle character-colored rim light.



Keep the lighting clean.



No dramatic environment lighting.



No cinematic hard shadows.





==================================================

25. FRONT LIGHT

==================================================



Use broad soft illumination across the character.



Keep:



face readable,

skin clean,

clothing silhouette clear.



Use gentle soft shading around:



neck,

under chin,

sides of torso,

fabric folds.



Do not flatten the character completely.





==================================================

26. CHARACTER-ADAPTIVE RIM LIGHT

==================================================



Add a subtle colored rim-light / edge glow derived from the target character's ACCENT COLOR.



Use it around selected portions of:



hair silhouette,

shoulder,

upper arm,

waist,

pants edge.



The effect should be restrained.



Think:



soft color contamination at the silhouette edge.



Not:



neon outline,

glowing sticker border,

cyberpunk aura.





==================================================

27. RIM LIGHT COLOR

==================================================



Automatically adapt the rim-light hue.



Examples:



blue character:

soft cyan / pale blue edge.



purple character:

muted violet edge.



red character:

soft crimson / rose edge.



green character:

muted mint / teal edge.



gold character:

soft warm amber edge.



Do not use pink by default.





==================================================

28. SKIN RENDERING

==================================================



Use smooth anime-style skin.



Use:



clean base tone,

soft controlled shadow,

slight cheek warmth only if appropriate.



No:



skin pores,

photographic texture,

sweat,

oily skin,

heavy blush,

grain.



Keep the face clean and stylized.





==================================================

29. HAIR RENDERING

==================================================



Preserve the canonical hairstyle.



Use:



large coherent hair locks,

clean silhouette,

few controlled highlights,

simple soft shadow structure.



Do not over-render strands.



Do not add random wind unless the chosen pose calls for slight movement.



This portrait is primarily static.





==================================================

30. CLOTHING MATERIAL

==================================================



The fitted upper garment may use:

smooth technical knit,

soft athletic fabric,

subtle satin-matte material.



The loose pants may use:



soft cotton,

smooth jersey,

light lounge fabric,

matte technical fabric.



Avoid strong leather gloss unless highly appropriate to character identity.



Do not use latex-like reflection.





==================================================

31. FABRIC FOLDS

==================================================



Use only structurally meaningful folds.



UPPER GARMENT:



very few folds due to fitted construction,

small tension around waist / side / shoulder.



LOWER GARMENT:



larger relaxed folds,

waist gathering,

soft vertical folds,

gentle volume around hips and thighs.



Avoid excessive micro-wrinkles.
